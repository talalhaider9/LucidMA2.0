LUCID-MA 2.0: Autonomous Multi-Agent Crime Analysis
Architected a high-efficiency upgrade from LUCID-MA (Learning and Understanding Crime
through Dialogue of Multiple Agents) to a 1.5B-parameter DeepSeek-R1 and multi-hop
GraphRAG with an early-stopping agent loop (10-epoch cap). Analyzed 2,979 assault records:
12.2% arrests, 26.7% domestic, peak 15:00; review halted after 2 epochs (VALID).
